Fees and terms below come from each provider's own UK pricing page, checked on 11 October 2026. Where a page does not state something, this guide says "not stated" rather than guessing. Prices change, so check the provider's page before signing up.

## Who this is for

This guide is for UK sole traders and very small trade businesses (plumbers, electricians, builders, decorators, gardeners, cleaners) who want to take card payments on the job or by a link sent to the customer. It compares five providers: SumUp, Square, Zettle by PayPal, Stripe and Dojo. It also covers whether hardware is needed at all.

## Free or low-cost option

Before paying for a card service, consider two lower-cost routes.

**Bank transfer with clear invoice details.** Send an invoice showing the business name, account name, sort code and account number, a payment reference, the amount due and the due date. The customer pays from their own banking app. Bank charges for receiving payments are not covered here; ask your bank. The limits are practical: the customer must be willing to type in details, the money should be seen to have arrived before leaving the job or releasing goods, and there is no card-style "pay now" moment on the doorstep.

**Phone-based tap to pay, where offered.** Some providers let you accept contactless cards on a compatible phone with no separate reader:

- Zettle by PayPal: Tap to Pay through the PayPal POS app, with "no additional hardware" and no separate fee listed (https://www.zettle.com/gb/pricing, checked 11 October 2026).
- Square: the in-person rate applies to card reader or Tap to Pay payments (https://squareup.com/gb/en/pricing, checked 11 October 2026).
- Dojo: Tap to Pay on iPhone costs £0 with "No contract"; its per-transaction fee is not stated (https://dojo.tech/pricing, checked 11 October 2026).
- Stripe: Tap to Pay is listed at +£0.10 per authorisation; the page does not state the percentage rate that goes with it (https://stripe.com/gb/pricing, checked 11 October 2026).
- SumUp: no separate tap to pay rate is given (https://www.sumup.com/en-gb/pricing/, checked 11 October 2026).

Honest limits: the pricing pages do not say which phones are supported, so check that with the provider before relying on it. The provider's transaction fee still applies. Phone-based payments depend on the phone's battery and signal, and a card reader may suit better if you take many payments in a day.

## The providers compared

### SumUp

Source: https://www.sumup.com/en-gb/pricing/ (checked 11 October 2026).

- Pay-as-you-go: 1.69% transaction fee, no monthly cost.
- Payments Plus: 0.99% transaction fee and £19 a month. The page says it suits businesses taking £3,000 or more a month.
- Custom: bespoke fees for £10,000 or more a month; no published rate.
- Separate rates for card reader, tap to pay, payment links or invoices: not given.
- Optional subscriptions: Invoices Plus £8 a month (excl. VAT); POS Plus £39 a month (excl. VAT); Business Account Plus £15 a month (incl. VAT).
- Card reader price: not stated. Payout timing: not stated. Contract terms: not stated.

### Square

Source: https://squareup.com/gb/en/pricing (checked 11 October 2026).

- In person (card reader or Tap to Pay): 1.75% for UK cards; non-UK cards 1.75% + 1.5%.
- Online (online store, checkout): 1.4% + 25p for UK cards; non-UK cards 2.5% + 25p. Payment links are not listed separately.
- Keyed-in payments: 2.5%. Invoices: 2.5% (non-UK cards + 1.5%).
- Hardware: Square Reader £19 + VAT. A lower promotional price was shown on the page for the Square Reader; it is not repeated here because it may be temporary. Square Terminal £149 + VAT, or £25 + VAT a month for 6 months.
- Monthly fees: Free plan £0 a month plus processing fees; Plus plan from £29 a month.
- Contract terms: not stated.
- Payouts: "Transfers as soon as the next day"; the FAQ says one to two working days are free, and instant transfers cost 1.5%.

### Zettle by PayPal

Source: https://www.zettle.com/gb/pricing (checked 11 October 2026).

- Card payments, including American Express and mobile wallets: 1.75% per transaction.
- Invoice: 2.5%. The page notes availability for sellers who signed up before 8 May 2023.
- Payment Links: 2.5%, but the page says they are temporarily unavailable for new sellers.
- Tap to Pay: through the PayPal POS app, "no additional hardware"; no separate fee listed.
- Card reader: from £29 excl. VAT for new business users only; additional readers £69 excl. VAT.
- Monthly fees and contract: "No contracts or recurring fees".
- Payouts: funds "typically arrive in your PayPal Business account within minutes"; no added fee to transfer to a bank.

### Stripe

Sources: https://stripe.com/gb/pricing and https://stripe.com/gb/terminal (both checked 11 October 2026).

- Online card payments: standard UK cards 1.5% + 20p; premium UK cards 2.8% + 20p; EEA cards 2.5% + 20p; international cards 3.15% + 20p; currency conversion +2%.
- Payment Links: included with Payments at no additional charge on standard pricing.
- Invoicing Starter: 0.4% per paid invoice, on top of the card fee.
- In person (Terminal): the page publishes two rates, **EEA cards 1.4% + 10p** and **non-EEA cards 2.9% + 10p**. The page does not say which applies to UK-issued cards, and the UK is not in the EEA. Both rates are therefore shown throughout this guide. Confirm with Stripe which one applies to your customers' UK cards before relying on either. Tap to Pay: +£0.10 per authorisation.
- Hardware cost: not stated on the pricing page.
- Monthly fees and contract: the FAQ says "Stripe does not charge setup fees, monthly fees, or any other hidden fees like closure fees" for standard payments.
- Payout timing: not stated.

### Dojo

Source: https://dojo.tech/pricing (checked 11 October 2026).

- In person, under £100,000 annual card turnover: 1.2% blended rate for all card transactions.
- Online or payment-link fee: payment links are listed as a feature; no separate fee is given.
- Invoice fee: not stated.
- Card machines, pay monthly: Dojo Go Max £25 a month (or £149 upfront, shown as a launch offer); Dojo Pocket £20 a month (or £239 upfront); Dojo Wired £15 a month (or £179 upfront).
- Tap to Pay on iPhone: £0, "No contract"; per-transaction fee not stated.
- Contract: over £100,000 turnover, 12-month fixed or 30-day rolling, with an early exit listed as "Contract buyout up to £3,000". Under £100,000: contract terms not stated.
- Payouts: "Get your takings next day".

## Worked examples

These are example calculations using published rates only. They are not a forecast of what you will pay. They do not cover VAT treatment, and rates and eligibility can differ from those shown, for example for non-UK or premium cards.

### Example 1: fees on a £200 job

Card fee only, with no monthly fees included.

| Provider and route | Arithmetic | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| SumUp pay-as-you-go | 1.69% of £200 = £3.38 | £3.38 |
| SumUp Payments Plus | 0.99% of £200 = £1.98 | £1.98 (plus £19 a month) |
| Square in person | 1.75% of £200 = £3.50 | £3.50 |
| Square invoice | 2.5% of £200 = £5.00 | £5.00 |
| Zettle card payment | 1.75% of £200 = £3.50 | £3.50 |
| Stripe online, standard UK card | 1.5% of £200 = £3.00; + 20p | £3.20 |
| Stripe in person (Terminal), if the EEA rate applies | 1.4% of £200 = £2.80; + 10p | £2.90 |
| Stripe in person (Terminal), if the non-EEA rate applies | 2.9% of £200 = £5.80; + 10p | £5.90 |
| Stripe invoice, standard UK card | £3.20 card fee + 0.4% of £200 (£0.80) | £4.00 |
| Dojo in person | 1.2% of £200 = £2.40 | £2.40 (plus machine cost) |

Stripe's page does not state which in-person rate applies to UK-issued cards, so both Stripe Terminal rows are shown and neither should be assumed. Stripe Tap to Pay, Dojo Tap to Pay on iPhone and Dojo payment links are not calculated, because no clear total rate is published for them.

### Example 2: monthly cost on £2,000 of card payments

For rates with a fixed amount per transaction, the £2,000 is assumed to be ten payments of £200. That split is only for the arithmetic. Monthly fees are included. One-off hardware purchases are not included.

| Provider and route | Arithmetic | Monthly total |
|---|---|---|
| SumUp pay-as-you-go | 1.69% of £2,000 = £33.80; no monthly fee | £33.80 |
| SumUp Payments Plus | 0.99% of £2,000 = £19.80; + £19 | £38.80 |
| Square in person, Free plan | 1.75% of £2,000 = £35.00; + £0 | £35.00 |
| Square online, Free plan | 1.4% of £2,000 = £28.00; + 10 × 25p (£2.50) | £30.50 |
| Zettle card payments | 1.75% of £2,000 = £35.00; no recurring fees | £35.00 |
| Stripe online, standard UK cards | 1.5% of £2,000 = £30.00; + 10 × 20p (£2.00) | £32.00 |
| Stripe in person (Terminal), if the EEA rate applies | 1.4% of £2,000 = £28.00; + 10 × 10p (£1.00) | £29.00 (plus any hardware; cost not stated) |
| Stripe in person (Terminal), if the non-EEA rate applies | 2.9% of £2,000 = £58.00; + 10 × 10p (£1.00) | £59.00 (plus any hardware; cost not stated) |
| Dojo with Dojo Wired (£15 a month) | 1.2% of £2,000 = £24.00; + £15 | £39.00 |
| Dojo with Dojo Pocket (£20 a month) | £24.00 + £20 | £44.00 |
| Dojo with Dojo Go Max (£25 a month) | £24.00 + £25 | £49.00 |

At £2,000 a month, SumUp's Payments Plus costs more than its pay-as-you-go plan (£38.80 against £33.80), which fits the page's own note that Payments Plus suits businesses taking £3,000 or more a month. Dojo's monthly machine cost offsets its lower percentage at this volume. Stripe's in-person total could be £29.00 or £59.00 depending on which published rate applies to UK-issued cards, a gap of £30.00 a month, and the page does not say which. Paying upfront instead (for example £179 for Dojo Wired) changes the comparison, and the page does not say how long a monthly arrangement runs for businesses under £100,000 turnover.

## How to choose

**In person or by link.** If most payments are taken on the doorstep, look at the in-person rates and whether a reader is needed. If customers mostly pay after the job, look at payment links and invoices. Stripe states that Payment Links are included at no additional charge on standard pricing. Zettle's Payment Links are temporarily unavailable to new sellers, and Square's page does not list payment links separately. For Stripe in person, two rates are published (1.4% + 10p for EEA cards, 2.9% + 10p for non-EEA cards) and the page does not say which covers UK-issued cards, so ask Stripe before comparing it with the others.

**Monthly volume.** Percentage fees matter more as volume grows; fixed monthly fees matter more when volume is low. In Example 2, totals at £2,000 a month ranged from £29.00 (Stripe in person at the EEA rate, hardware excluded) to £59.00 (Stripe in person at the non-EEA rate, hardware excluded); among the rows with a single published rate, the range was £30.50 to £49.00. These are examples, not a ranking for any particular business. Work through the same arithmetic with your own figures.

**Hardware or phone.** Zettle states "no additional hardware" for Tap to Pay, and Dojo lists Tap to Pay on iPhone at £0. For a physical reader, the stated prices are Square Reader £19 + VAT (with a lower promotional price shown), Zettle from £29 excl. VAT (new business users), and Dojo machines from £15 a month. SumUp's and Stripe's hardware prices are not stated on their pricing pages.

**Contracts.** Zettle states no contracts or recurring fees. Stripe's FAQ states no monthly fees on standard payments. Square's Free plan is £0 a month, though contract terms are not stated. Dojo states contract terms only for businesses above £100,000 turnover; under that, ask Dojo directly before agreeing.

**Payout speed.** Dojo states next-day takings. Zettle states funds typically arrive in your PayPal Business account within minutes. Square states as soon as the next day, with one to two working days free. SumUp's and Stripe's payout timing is not stated on their pricing pages. If cash is needed quickly for materials, this may decide it.

**Card types.** Several pages charge more for some cards. Square charges non-UK cards an extra 1.5% in person. Stripe's online rates rise for premium UK, EEA and international cards, and add 2% for currency conversion. Zettle states American Express and mobile wallets are included in its 1.75%. Dojo's 1.2% is described as a blended rate for all card transactions. If customers are almost all UK individuals paying with ordinary cards, the headline rates are closer to what will be paid, but for Stripe in person the page does not say which headline rate that is.
