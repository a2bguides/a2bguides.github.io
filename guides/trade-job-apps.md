This is an independent comparison of five job-management apps that UK trades use for quotes, scheduling and invoices: Tradify, Jobber, ServiceM8, Fergus and Powered Now. It uses only facts taken from each vendor's own pricing page. Prices and features were taken from those pages on **11 October 2026**, and the URLs are listed in the Sources section. We have not tested the apps, and the pages may have changed since that date, so confirm everything on the vendor's page before you sign up.

Where a page does not say something, we write "not stated" rather than guess. That does not mean the feature is absent.

## Who this is for

This guide is for solo traders and very small teams in the UK, such as plumbers, electricians, builders, decorators, gardeners and cleaners, who are deciding whether to pay for a first job-management app and, if so, which one to look at.

It does not assume you need an app. Many one-person businesses can run on a simple spreadsheet for a while. The next section covers that first, then the apps.

## Free or basic option

The cheapest way to start is a spreadsheet and a quote template, plus a way to capture enquiries so none get lost.

We publish a free enquiry logger that sends a Google Form into a Google Sheet: https://a2bguides.github.io/guides/enquiry-logger/ (checked 11 October 2026). Pair it with a quote template in a word processor or spreadsheet, and an invoice template, and you have a working basic system at no software cost.

**What the free option does:** it records who got in touch, what they wanted and when.

**What it does not do:** the logger records enquiries only. It does not produce quotes, schedule jobs, send invoices, take card payments or connect to accounting software. Quotes and invoices from templates are filled in and sent by hand, and you chase payments yourself.

**When an app may become worth paying for.** We cannot put a number on this, because we have no measured figures and will not invent any. Questions that point towards an app:

- Are you re-typing the same customer and job details into a quote, then an invoice?
- Do you need customers to pay by card or online link, and do templates make that awkward?
- Do you now have a second person whose diary needs to line up with yours?
- Does your bookkeeping need quotes and invoices to flow into accounting software such as Xero or QuickBooks?

If the answer to all of these is no, a spreadsheet may be enough for now, and paying monthly for an app is not necessary. If one or two are yes, a free trial costs nothing to try (every vendor below states a 14-day trial on its page).

## The apps compared

All figures are from each vendor's pricing page, checked 11 October 2026. "Cheapest plan for one person" means the lowest-priced plan the page shows that suits a single user.

### Tradify

- **Page:** https://www.tradifyhq.com/uk/pricing (checked 11 October 2026)
- **Cheapest plan:** Lite, £34 per user per month (GBP). Pro is £37, Plus is £44, and Custom is "Price by request".
- **VAT:** not stated.
- **Lite includes:** quoting and invoicing; scheduling with Google Calendar sync; online and credit card payments; accounting sync with Xero, Sage and QuickBooks Online.
- **Free trial:** 14 days with all features.
- **Contract:** the page says "No contracts, no commitments, cancel anytime", and its FAQ describes month-to-month billing.
- **Mobile app:** the FAQ says it can be downloaded for iPhone, iPad and Android.
- **Add-ons:** Instant Website £10 per month; text message quotes and invoices £0.10 per message.

### Jobber

- **Page:** https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/ (checked 11 October 2026)
- **Currency:** US dollars only. The page shows no pound price, so the cost in pounds is not stated.
- **Cheapest plan:** Core, one user: $49 per month with no commitment, $39 per month with a one-year commitment, or $29 per month billed annually.
- **Core includes:** online booking and scheduling of jobs; professional quotes; invoicing with online payments; a professional website; built-in reporting.
- **Next plan:** Connect, one user: $139 per month with no commitment, $119 with a one-year commitment, $99 billed annually. It adds automated client reminders, automatic payment collection, quote and invoice follow-ups, QuickBooks Online sync, and time and expense tracking. Extra users are $29 per month each.
- **Free trial:** 14 days, no credit card required.
- **Accounting:** QuickBooks Online sync is confirmed in the FAQ and listed under Connect. Xero sync is not stated.
- **Mobile app:** not stated on the pricing page.
- **VAT:** not stated.

### ServiceM8

- **Page:** https://www.servicem8.com/pricing (checked 11 October 2026)
- **Currency:** the page says "Prices are in USD".
- **Plans:** Free, $0 per month, 1 user and 30 jobs per month. Starter, $29 per month, unlimited users and 50 jobs per month. Growing, $79 per month, 150 jobs. Premium, $149 per month, 500 jobs.
- **Cheapest plan for one person:** Free, with a limit of 30 jobs per month. Starter is the next step up. Whether the prices are monthly-only or have an annual alternative is not stated, and VAT is not stated.
- **Free trial:** 14 days.
- **All plans include:** quoting and invoicing; scheduling; payments through the app or an online link; sync with Xero, QuickBooks Online and MYOB; a mobile app.
- **Mobile platforms:** the page names iOS and Android in one place and only iOS in another, so Android support is unclear. If you use an Android phone, check this before relying on it.
- **Contract and cancellation:** not stated on the pricing page.

### Fergus

- **Page:** https://www.fergus.com/uk/pricing/ (checked 11 October 2026)
- **Cheapest plan:** Essentials, "From £39 per month" (GBP). Professional is "From £45 per month".
- **Temporary promotion:** on the day we checked, the page showed those prices struck through and reduced to £3.90 and £4.50 under the banner "Limited Offer: Get 90% off til December". This is a time-limited offer, so treat £39 as the list price and check what applies when you sign up.
- **Billing:** monthly, with "no lock-in contracts". Annual pricing is not confirmed by the page. VAT is not stated.
- **Free trial:** 14 days, "no credit card required".
- **Both plans include:** quoting and estimating; a calendar; invoicing; payments; a mobile app; Xero, QuickBooks and MYOB connections.
- **Support level:** Essentials has email and chat support only; Professional adds phone.
- **Payment fees:** credit card payments cost 1.6% + £0.20 per transaction. SMS alerts cost £8 per 100.

### Powered Now

- **Page:** https://www.powerednow.com/pricing (checked 11 October 2026)
- **Cheapest plan:** Business (single user), £28 per month, or £280 charged once a year (GBP, excluding VAT). Professional is £32 per month per user or £320 per user per year. Premium is £40 per month or £400 per user per year. The page describes annual billing as "two months free".
- **Free trial:** 14 days, no card required.
- **All plans include:** quotes and estimates; diary and appointments; invoices and part invoices; online payments and payment links; apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and PC.
- **Accounting:** Xero and QuickBooks Online integration is **not** included on Business. It is included on Professional and Premium. A sole trader who wants accounting sync therefore needs to compare the £32 Professional price, not the £28 Business price.
- **Contract and cancellation:** not stated on the pricing page.

## How to choose

Ask these questions, then check the answers against the facts above. We do not rank the apps; the right fit depends on your answers.

1. **How many people will use it?** Tradify, Jobber and Powered Now quote per user for their entry plans. ServiceM8's Free plan covers one user, while its Starter plan lists unlimited users but caps jobs per month. If you may add a second person, look at how each price changes. Jobber's extra users are $29 per month each on Connect.
2. **How many jobs do you run?** ServiceM8's plans are limited by jobs per month (30 on Free, 50 on Starter, 150 on Growing). The other four pricing pages do not state a job cap.
3. **Do you need quotes in the app?** All five pages list quoting.
4. **Do you want customers to pay by card?** Tradify lists online and credit card payments on Lite. Fergus states a card fee of 1.6% + £0.20 per transaction. ServiceM8, Jobber and Powered Now list online payments, but the pages we read give no fee details, so check them on the vendor's site.
5. **Which accounting software do you already use?** If it is Xero, Jobber's pricing page does not state support. If it is QuickBooks, note that Jobber's sync sits on Connect and Powered Now's sits on Professional. If you use something else, check the lists above (Sage is named only by Tradify; MYOB by ServiceM8 and Fergus).
6. **What currency do you want to pay in?** Tradify, Fergus and Powered Now show pounds. Jobber and ServiceM8 show US dollars.
7. **What are the contract and cancellation terms?** Tradify and Fergus state no contract or lock-in. Jobber offers no-commitment, one-year and annual prices at different rates. Powered Now offers monthly and annual prices. ServiceM8's terms are not stated on its pricing page. Read the vendor's terms before paying, and check the VAT position, which most pages do not state.
8. **Which phone do you use?** Confirm the app works on your device, particularly on Android for ServiceM8, and check Jobber's mobile app, which the pricing page does not mention.
9. **Do you need an app yet?** If not, stay with the free option above and revisit later.
