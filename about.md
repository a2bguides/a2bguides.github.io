**A2B Guides** publishes self-service guides for solo service businesses and freelancers.

## How the guides are made

Each guide is drafted with AI assistance. It then goes through automatic checks and an
independent review that opens every cited source. A person approves it before it's
published. Prices and features are taken from vendors' own pages and dated. When
something can't be verified, the guide says so.

## Referrals

We may, in future, earn a commission if you buy a product through some links in our
guides. Any such link will be clearly labelled as an ad, next to the link itself.
Our recommendations do not depend on commission, and we recommend a free option
wherever it's enough. At present, no guide on this site contains referral links.

## Support

These guides are self-service. We can't offer personal setup help. For help with a
product, use that vendor's own help pages, which are linked in each guide.

## Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, please [report a correction]({{ site.corrections_form }}).
The form asks for a name, an email address and your message. We use these only to
deal with the correction, and we don't publish them.
