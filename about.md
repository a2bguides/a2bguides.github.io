A2B Guides publishes practical articles and gift ideas for people with niche hobbies. Facts about safety, rules
and seasons come from the organisations that run or advise on each hobby, and each article lists its sources
with the date they were checked.

The site is run by [A2B Designs](https://www.etsy.com/shop/A2BDesignsUK), a small UK shop that makes gifts for the same hobbies. Some articles
mention our own designs where they fit; those links go to our Etsy shop and say so. We do not take payment to
feature anything else.
