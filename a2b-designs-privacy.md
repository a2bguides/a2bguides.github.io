This page covers the A2B Guides website and the A2B Designs shop tools (13 October 2026).

## What we collect

- **This website** counts page visits with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies or collect personal data.
- **A2B Designs** sells on Etsy (shop A2BDesignsUK) and Redbubble. Orders, payments and buyer details are handled by
  those marketplaces and by our print partner Printify, under their own privacy policies. We only see what an order
  needs (name and delivery address) and use it only to fulfil that order.
- **Pinterest and Etsy tools.** Our own small, private tools use the official Pinterest and Etsy APIs to post pins of
  our products on our Pinterest account and to read our own shop's views and sales. They do not collect data about
  other people, and nothing is sold or shared.

## Your choices

To ask about or remove any data we hold, message us through our Etsy shop. We reply within 30 days.
